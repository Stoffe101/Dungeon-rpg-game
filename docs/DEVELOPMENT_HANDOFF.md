owner, 2026-11-02 to 2026-11-07), the repository, its 126 commits, 11 pull requests
and CI history. It exists so a fresh session, in particular one on Linux-based

`CLAUDE.md` holds the standing rules and commands. This file holds the history and
context behind them. The other docs go deeper: `docs/DESIGN.md` (systems and their
reasons), `docs/STATUS.md` (validation per pass), `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`,
`docs/NETWORK.md`, `docs/STEAM.md`, `docs/TESTING.md` (rules, tools, human checklists).

| Term | Meaning |

**Four things that will bite a new session**

1. **`main` is empty.** It has one commit and three files (`.gitattributes`,
   `.gitignore`, `README.md`). No game and no `CLAUDE.md`. All work sits in the PR stack,
   125 commits ahead of `main`. A cloud session must start from
   `feature/enemy-presentation`, not the default branch. Never "fix" this by merging:
   the owner reviews and merges.
2. **New work goes on a new branch stacked on the latest**, with a draft PR whose base
   is the previous branch. Never merge the stack, never enable auto-merge.
3. **Rendering may not work in the cloud.** Headless tests do (proven by CI). Captures
   need a real renderer, and the owner treats looking at the game as mandatory. Test
   this first (section 9). If it cannot be made to work, say so in every report.
4. **The owner is the only human tester.** Bots never prove feel. Passes #9 to #11 hold
   a lot of unplayed work, so the most useful next event is a human session, not more
   features (section 8).

## 1. Vision, identity and major decisions

### Where it came from

On 2026-11-02 the owner gave a "Master Development Prompt" for an ambitious 2.5D
action roguelite with deep incremental RPG progression, naming no setting. Name, lore
and world were left to Claude, which created Kindled Deep. Each brief since has
restated the same stance: Claude is the lead creative director and developer of an
autonomous one-team studio, not someone working a checklist.

### The identity that must survive

- **Mostly active combat.** The player moves, aims, attacks, dodges, wards and casts by
  hand. Never an idle or auto-combat game.
- **Light is the theme.** The sun died. The Kindled carries hearth-fire in a lantern into
  the Undervault. Warm ember means the player; cold teal soul-light means the Undervault.
  Kindle is both the spell resource and the lantern's reach.
- **The dungeon remembers.** Grudges, the Chronicle, Undervault Memory. This is the
  signature.
- **Builds, not numbers.** Things should change how you fight.
- **Atmospheric dark fantasy pixel art.** "I like the current dark fantasy direction",
  but readable: not bright, flat or cartoonish.
- **Solo stays first-class; co-op for up to four is a real pillar.** The owner plays
  with their girlfriend, and that shapes much of what matters.

The long-term ambition is 100+ hours of enjoyable play, from gameplay depth and "not
artificial grinding". Never claim this has been achieved: nobody can know yet.

### Decisions with their reasons

The full list is in `docs/DESIGN.md` under "Decisions log". The ones that most need
their reason kept:

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| 3D world with sprites, not pure 2D | Real lights and shadows are central to the light theme. World z and y are stretched by sqrt(2) so the 45-degree camera shows art 1:1. |
| All art and audio generated by code in `tools/` | No external tools were available. Reproducible, diffable, consistent. |
| Grudges instead of a plain death penalty | Loss becomes a goal: what killed you carries your Embers. |
| Hearthtree mastery ranks and a level cap of 60 | The owner worried players feel done once a tree is full, so builds must matter. |
| Host-authoritative co-op with client prediction | Combat outcomes stay fair and cheat-resistant; the guest's own movement stays responsive. |
| Each player keeps their own progression | A party session must never corrupt anyone's save or sanctuary. |
| Shared income is equal for all, gain bonuses are the party's | From the Lv 12 versus Lv 10 report. Equal starts must end equal. |
| Gear finds are private and banked when taken | From the owner: loot they can wear at once and that only they can see. |
| Sealed rooms use an arena and a watchdog, never a timer | Nothing opens because time passed; challenge and rewards stay honest. |
| Foes take turns (two wind-ups per Kindled) | Crowds stay dangerous but readable, without lowering damage. |
| Answers at Hollow Altars are always reversible | A permanent penalty punishes experimenting. Rejected: a corruption meter that only worsens. |
| An expedition's world is the host's | Guests' own altars stay untouched; they only witness. |
| Readability is art in the sheet (a rim per region), not a shader | The owner: do not "solve every visual problem with shader changes". The rim differs by region and goes out when a foe dies. |
| A guardian's spoils wait for its fall | Looking at the fall showed loot text buried the best moment of a run. |
| Host migration rejected for now | Risk to a reliable floor is larger than its value while checkpoints and rejoin work. |
| No puzzle, escort or collapsing rooms | Softlock and stale-on-repeat risk. |

### Decisions that were reversed (do not bring these back)

- **Mouse-guided steering.** The owner asked for it (second playtest), it shipped in
  PR #8, the owner tried it and asked for it to be removed: "I would prefer to abandon that
  experimental movement scheme." WASD always moves in screen space. The mouse only
  aims and turns the face.
- **The guardian edge arrow.** Removed by request: "annoying and unnecessary."
- **A bigger shared-loot design.** Personal loot is private by explicit requirement.
- **Facing only the mouse.** It made walking away look like moonwalking.
  The Kindled faces its walk and turns to the aim when fighting.

Causes are from the commit messages and docs of the time. Where the cause is unknown,

### Found by a human, missed by every automated test

**Owner solo, 2026-11-02 and 11-03**

| Symptom | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Character could only attack one way | Player lives in the world SubViewport, which receives no input, so the mouse position was stale. It had never worked for a human. | Read the mouse from the PixelView in the root window (`75ffccd`) |
| No sign of the flask | Never drawn | Flask slot on the hotbar |
| Footsteps did not match walking | Played every 1.6 tiles | Played on the run cycle's contact frames |
| Character did not turn with WASD | Sprite only ever faced the mouse | `322c582`, later fixed properly in `23b55e2` |
| Charged shot "sounds like a guitar string" | The bow used the plucked-string synth | New knock, whoosh and whistle |
| Satchel looked like a leather bag | Art. The open view was praised, so it was kept | Exterior redrawn |

**First two-PC playtest, 2026-11-03.** Every test had passed. All of it was visible on
someone's screen, which is why `docs/TESTING.md` exists.

| Symptom | Root cause |
|---|---|

### Found by real windows and processes

- **A thorn cut on the host stayed solid on the guest's machine.** Prop state travelled without solidity, so
  an obstacle could be open on one machine and shut on another. Found only by `scenario=depthworld`.

- Foes' bars and nameplates lingering on guests' screens after they fell.
- A party starting a floor behind a pillar.
- A rejoined guest had no checkpoint if the host then left.
- Other Kindled could wall a player in at the sanctuary.
- Losing window focus popped a "Paused" menu that paused nothing.
- **Exported builds could not start:** `looks.gd` preloaded the palette from `tools/`,
  which exports omit. The palette is now `src/render/pal.gd`, and `release_check.py` fails
  on this.
- Quitting while hosting on Steam crashed: Steam shut down under a live peer.

### Found by measuring

- **The Thousandth Cut** came after 48 s in small fights, 18 times a minute in crowds
  (`-- legendlab`). Rebuilt to about 2.5 and 4.5 a minute.
- **A party's closing wave still paid**, lifting XP to 1.36-1.78x solo. It now pays
  nothing (`-- economy`).
- **Tempo bonus** written into `attack_rate` was lost at guardian phase changes and
  shortened telegraphs. It is now separate `tempo`.
- **Ambient one-shots** were heard twice by guests.
- **Trial rooms took 65 s** once seals counted as foes: too much health, and the bot
  ground through warded foes. Bot behaviour matters as much as the numbers.
- **The Tollkeeper** was the only new foe that clearly moved difficulty. Comparing the same
  seeds with and without it stopped a false blame on the Ash Herald.
- **The Throne King "beat every bot" (0 of 40, 2026-11-08)** because his shockwave, sun-pillars and
  floor spikes noted no danger and the bot never sheltered from the Eclipse; deaths read "The
  Undervault". With the warnings shared (no damage changed): 16 of 20 without gear, 19 of 20 with
  Tier I gear (`tools/dev/bossbench.py`). Read a death's last blows before touching numbers.
- **The Gravecleaver** staggered whole packs every swing and took a twentieth of the damage
  other swords took (`-- weaponlab`); its cleaves' poise came down. **Unblinking** made the
  slowest build of eleven; its penalty halved.
- **The bow "weak against guardians" (2026-11-08)** was the bot: `use: quick 215, drawn 0%` for one build,
  draws let go during recovery for another. Read what a build actually did before tuning it.
- **`kit=`** equipped a random kind of weapon, so a bench "with gear" once fought with the wrong
  weapon. It now keeps the build's weapon.

### Found by looking at captures (2026-11-05)

- **A guardian's death was buried** under four loot labels and stacked titles.
- Rock past a wall had no mesh, so caps ended in black rectangles.
- Foes melted into floors: black outlines vanish on dark stone and the warm lantern browns
  every colour. The pale Husk rendered dark brown.
- Champions had no nameplate on any machine (promoted after their first frame).
- Banners collided with world labels. Toasts covered the controls card.
- The third depths drew a bright grid (a seam glow that predated the bigger paving), and
  wall marks read as the letter K.

### Found by reading code

- **Every ring and slash reached guests twice**, once as a still full-size copy, because
  `Fx.ring` and `Fx.swipe` recorded themselves and then called `shape`, which recorded again.
- `Profile.stockpile()` was true in **debug exports**, so **the zips sent for playtests
  gave out the testing stockpile**. It now needs the editor. Treat earlier human impressions
  of economy and pacing with that in mind.

### Lessons

1. **Tests prove what they exercise, and nothing else.** The bot called `prop.interact()`
   directly, so Interact could have no input path and every test still passed.
2. **The host's picture proves nothing about the guest's.** Run separate processes and
   look at each side.
3. **Look at the game.** Capture it and view it after anything visible. The biggest
   presentation problems of the last pass were found this way.
4. **A failing test may be the test.** Two guardian failures under latency were my
   scenario's own timing on lagged guests. It was rewritten to measure on each machine's
   clock. Find out which it is; do not guess either way.
5. **Bots measure shape, not truth.** They never Ward against melee or dodge rings, and
   they only do objectives you teach them. Compare like with like.
6. **Keep failure logs.** A 4-player failure on 2026-11-05 lost its log to the next run,
   so its cause is still unknown.
7. **Find the cause before the fix.** Every report is a symptom. The owner asks for
   this explicitly, and "do not simply reduce the overlay opacity" is the model.

## 4. Current systems and how they interact

Layers: `src/core` (rules) -> `src/data` (tables) -> `src/world` -> `src/entities` ->
`src/combat` -> `src/render` -> `src/ui`. Autoloads: Events, Settings, Profile, Audio,
Game. Details in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**The loop.** Sanctuary (prepare) -> Gate (region, tier, Omens) -> floors, with a Forked
Descent at about 70% of stairs -> guardian -> home through a Hearthstone, Hearthcall or
death -> spend at Hearthtree, Forge, Apothecary, residents -> again, with the world changed.

**Flows that cross systems.** Break one of these and bugs appear far from the change.

| Flow | Path |
|---|---|
| Damage out | Everything through `Player.deal_hit`, including weapon forms, runes, uniques and the seal and Grudge wards |
| Damage in | Everything through `Player.take_enemy_hit`: dodge, ward, stance, bastion, marks, barrier, in that order |
| Gear | Any save change -> `Profile.changed` -> `Player.sync_gear`. Never refresh from a screen. |
| Sanctuary change | `Hub.refresh_camp` by tagged group. Never `Game.goto_hub()` for an upgrade. |
| Shared income | `Party.award_xp` / `award_embers` only, equal for all, recorded by `Ledger` |
| Personal finds | `Pickup._take_find` -> `Profile.bank_find`; guests via `NetRun.give` / `_gift`, once by drop id; events as `["own", peer, ev]` |
| Foe placement | `stage.foe_spot` inside `Dungeon.arena_of`; `_watch_arena` brings strays back |
| Foe attacks | Must get a turn from `may_strike()` |
| Room objectives | `Room.objective` -> `_begin_objective` / `_tend_objective`; HUD line is `Dungeon.objective`, cleared in `_room_cleared` |
| Host effects to guests | `Fx`, `Audio` and spawns record through `Net.rec`; wrap with `Net.mute` what clients do themselves |
| Guardian death | `die` -> `_on_actor_died` hands `_spoils_of` to `Enemy.spoils` when there is a view -> `guardian_fall` plays, burns, then drops. Headless drops at once. |
| World memory | `Profile.data.chronicle` -> `Chronicle.snapshot()` carried in the run -> `DungeonGen.generate(..., world)` -> floors differ; guests read the host's |
| Generation | Deterministic per seed. Use the passed rng or `_shuffle`, never `Array.shuffle()` or `randf()`. |

**Who owns what in a party**

| Owner | What |
|---|---|
| Host | Simulation, world, foes, floor, sanctuary camp, Chronicle sites for the expedition |
| Each player | Level, gear, Hearthtree, currencies, personal finds, Grudges, Pale Bowl marks |
| Party vote | Stairs and routes, Hearthstone, Trial, Hearthcall, altars, coffers |
| Local only | Cosmetics, Settings such as Reduce Flashes (applied on each machine) |

**Rendering.** A low-resolution 3D world in `PixelView`, upscaled. `LevelBuilder` feeds
`world.gdshader` (UV2 bits, COLOR = depth into rock). Sprites use `sprite.gdshader`; it and
`silhouette.gdshader` pin instance uniforms to the same slots, and must stay so. Sheets are
PNG, JSON sidecar and optional `_emit.png`, all generated.

## 6. Known issues, debt and limitations

**Open and unexplained**

- **Frame stall on ultrawide: resolved.** It was the test bot's debug dump through a terminal
  pipe, not the game (see STATUS). Never measure frame times with output piped.
- **Keeper click: fixed in the Great Evolution pass.** A click out of reach silently became an
  attack (the old test's comment claimed a walk-then-talk that did not exist). It now walks over
  and talks; `tests/test_shift.gd` covers the walk and keys taking over.

**Balance is unproven everywhere.** Numbers are first guesses or bot-derived: new foes,
routes, seals, flame drain, tinctures, Omens, Echoes, Tier II Throne (bots wipe with or
without the Herald), co-op difficulty. A guardian's 3 s spoils delay: pause or wait? Ask people.

**Co-op economy.** The owner said not to change multipliers from noisy bot numbers. Use
`-- economy` (exact). Measured: XP per Kindled 1.10-1.16x (2 players), 1.23-1.28x (3),
1.34-1.44x (4); Embers per Kindled over a floor 1.04-1.10x, 1.12-1.17x, 1.19-1.26x.
Whether that feels fair is for play.

**Limitations**

- No host migration: a lost host ends the expedition; guests keep their checkpoint.
- No real Steam App ID, so release builds have Steam off (needs a Steamworks account).
- Steam overlay and launch-from-invite need the game started through Steam.
- Licensed creature sounds are excluded from exports, so exports use generated sounds.
- Armour overlays are 32x32, tiny pieces read as colour. Death frames show no armour.
- Growing guardian shapes show at their start size on guests.
- Equipment bonuses on `TEST_STOCKPILE`: the constant is still `true`; `stockpile()` gates it
  to the editor.

**Debt**

- `docs/STATUS.md` is an append-only log and contains old statements, for example
  about pointer steering in the "Playtest foundations" entry. Trust the newest entry.
- `README.md` was brought up to date in the Great Evolution pass; keep its feature list current.

## 9. Linux and Claude Code Cloud

Everything before now ran on the owner's Windows PC with a real GPU, window and Steam.
The following is split by evidence, because **no session has yet run on Linux with a window**.

### Proven on Linux

GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/tests.yml`, ubuntu-latest) runs on every push. The last
40 runs on all 11 branches succeeded, and the run for `6b4e155` reported
`219 tests, 0 failures`. On that run, import took about 12 s and tests about 3 minutes.

A scan of every literal `res://` path in the repo found **no wrong-case paths** and no
case collisions, so the Windows-to-Linux filesystem change should be safe. Line endings are
normalised to LF by `.gitattributes`.

### Setup (from CI, so known good)

```bash
git fetch origin && git checkout feature/enemy-presentation   # not main: see section 0
export GODOT_VERSION=4.7.2
mkdir -p ~/godot
curl -sSL -o /tmp/godot.zip "https://github.com/godotengine/godot/releases/download/${GODOT_VERSION}-stable/Godot_v${GODOT_VERSION}-stable_linux.x86_64.zip"
unzip -q /tmp/godot.zip -d ~/godot
chmod +x ~/godot/Godot_v${GODOT_VERSION}-stable_linux.x86_64
export GODOT=~/godot/Godot_v${GODOT_VERSION}-stable_linux.x86_64
timeout 300 "$GODOT" --headless --path . --import || true   # CI tolerates a non-zero exit here
```

Whether the cloud's network allows that download is unknown. If it does not, ask the
owner to allow it or provide the binary. The Windows path in `CLAUDE.md` does not apply.
Use `$GODOT`, and always wrap Godot in `timeout`: a script error can hang it.

```bash
run() { n=$1; s=$2; d=$3; shift 3; DEVNET=$d LIMIT=600 bash tools/dev/e2e.sh "$n" "$s" "$@" > /dev/null 2>&1; echo "== $n $s $*"; grep -h RESULT captures/e2e/*.log; grep -h "FAIL " captures/e2e/*.log | head -8; mkdir -p keep && cp captures/e2e/*.log keep/; }
run 4 journey 0
run 3 journey 0
run 2 journey 0 typical
run 2 bestiary 1
run 4 guardian 1 typical typical typical
run 3 guardian 1 moderate moderate
run 4 sites 1 typical
run 2 loot 1 typical
run 2 stairs 1 typical
run 2 partystart 0
```

Keep logs per run. `e2e.sh` deletes the previous logs, which is how the Keeper failure's log
was lost. `DEVNET=1` is needed by scenarios that start after the party has formed.

**Save migration check.** Copy each old save to `user://mig_<v>.json`, run
`-- scene=dungeon region=ossuary seed=5 profile=mig_<v>.json frames=40 quit`, then read the
file: `version` must be 15, `chronicle` present, and embers, sunshards, level, skills and
grudges unchanged. A sanctuary load does **not** write the save; starting an expedition does.
In the editor, saves from v3 and v4 gain the stockpile, which never happens in an export.

**Aim a capture at a guardian's fall**

```bash
timeout 100 "$GODOT" --path . --resolution 1920x1080 -- scene=dungeon profile=cap.json godmode autopilot region=ossuary depth=3 seed=5 warp=boss bossdie=3 capture=probe frames=2400 quit | grep "guardian falls"
```

**Compare with and without a foe.** Temporarily remove it from `EnemyDB.REGIONS[...].pool`,
run `-- scaling routes= sizes=1 comps=mixed,melee,ranged seeds=2 secs=170 tier=2 region=<r> depth=2`
on both, restore. Same seeds or the numbers mean nothing.

**Static sound check.** Scan `Audio.play*("name"` literals in `src/` against
`assets/audio/sfx` and `music`. At handoff every name resolved. After changing sounds, check

**Parse-check one script.** `--check-only` output cascades; filter it:


A real error in any script shows up as a wall of "Could not resolve class".

## Appendix B: traps in this codebase

- Parse-time cycles: use untyped params across mutually dependent scripts (`DeepEvents` takes
  an untyped `lay`). Avoid `Enemy.CONST` from `dungeon.gd`.
- `-s` tool scripts cannot see autoloads or `class_name`s the same way; `preload` helpers, as
  `gen_depths.gd` does with `GenStone`. A failed preload still prints "built", so check output.
- Guardians hold a phase gate: a big hit triggers the phase, it does not kill.
  `bossdie=` calls `die` directly for this reason.
- `Fx.ring` eases out. Anything that must hit where a ring is drawn uses the same curve
  (`Tollkeeper.ring_radius`).
- `Stage.after` runs on the sim clock, so slow-motion stretches it. Tweens use real time.
- A find's title is private, so a delayed one must re-mute (`Stage.find_title`).
- `Enemy` names can clash: `_aura_t` already belonged to an affix, hence `_shed_t`.
- Capturing with the mouse away from centre shifts the camera, which leads toward the cursor.
- `e2e.sh` needs `DEVNET=1` for `loot`, `stairs`, `sites`, `guardian`, `bestiary`, and
  `DEVNET=0` for `journey`, `partystart`.
- Adding a foe changes encounters for existing seeds; tests that fell a wave once can flake
  on burrowed gravemoles.
